# Shared Anti-Patterns

These MUST-NOT patterns apply to every skill unless a specific skill explicitly documents a narrower boundary:

- Do not guess missing inputs, evidence, or requirements — stop and ask.
- Do not present an unreviewed artifact as final; the Review In Mind loop always runs first.
- Do not mix review findings with new design suggestions unless the skill says otherwise.
- Do not claim success without evidence (a passing check, a valid artifact, or an explicit known gap).
- Do not invent tools, files, or commands that are not defined by the skill or the workspace.
- Do not silently skip a stop rule because the answer "seems obvious".
- Do not let a failed attempt loop forever; respect the retry budget or stop rule.
